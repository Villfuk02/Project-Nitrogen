## The stuff
You are a hero pilot that goes towards the centre of hte galaxy to save the previous scientific expedition (or what remains).
When you finally get there, you realize they are dead.
You get to run the experiments yourself. By running them, you ship is sent straight into the blackhole.
On the next playthrough, you can override the computer to instead stay, and leave a message for others.
On the next, you find your own ship and realize this is sus and you are probably a clone
(Dutring playthroughs, you get hints in events that something is wrong)
Every time you get there, you have several years to work on your master plan to get revenge to the company that is cloning you
Then, you finally go back and destroy them, saving your clones from this hell.
